Who We Are

A Symphony
Born from Botswana

Founded in 2025 by a passionate group of musicians and music educators, the Gaborone Community Orchestra has quickly become one of Botswana's most celebrated ensembles. We bring together musicians of all backgrounds — from students to seasoned professionals — united by a love of music.

Our mission is to make orchestral music accessible, vibrant, and deeply woven into the cultural fabric of Gaborone. We perform across schools, community centres, and concert halls throughout the year.
